# Listening evaluation protocol

Evaluate after rendering. Do not score the written prompt alone.

Each dimension is scored from 1 to 5:

- **source retention** — can the source structure or material still be perceived?
- **musical value** — does the result work as music beyond demonstrating a mapping?
- **singularity** — does it feel specific to this session rather than interchangeable?
- **causal legibility** — can a listener/researcher trace major decisions back to documented inputs?
- **relisten desire** — is there a reason to hear the piece again?

## Decision thresholds

- any score 1 → `reject` or major `revise`;
- mean < 3.0 → `revise`;
- mean 3.0–3.79 → `keep_experimental`;
- mean ≥ 3.8 with source retention ≥ 4 and singularity ≥ 4 → eligible for `promote_candidate`.

A fertile accident is documented separately. It does not become a rule until it succeeds across additional sessions.
