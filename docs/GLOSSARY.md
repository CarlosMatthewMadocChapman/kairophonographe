# Glossary

**Kairophonographe** — system that translates a situated moment into traceable musical decisions.

**Music ADN** — a compact set of musical behaviours derived from the forces of a session and allowed to transform through a composition.

**Force** — an observed or derived property with a musical consequence: density, pressure, flow, friction, recurrence, openness, rupture, depth.

**Gesture** — an audible action performed by the composition: reveal, connect, stratify, condense, protect silence, etc.

**Resonance palette** — a family of timbral/structural responses chosen from physical forces, not geographic stereotypes.

**Kinetic regime** — the dominant organisation of pulse and movement.

**Vocal transmutation** — conversion of source timing/contour/density into non-lexical human vocal behaviour while preserving traceability.

**Reference example** — reproducible fixture used for explanation or regression; it does not govern new generations.
