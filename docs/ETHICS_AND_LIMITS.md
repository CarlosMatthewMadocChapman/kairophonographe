# Ethics and limits

## No geographic essentialism

A territory never automatically selects a national, ethnic or folkloric style. Physical forces may select timbres and structures; cultural material enters only with documented provenance and an explicit artistic reason.

## No false sensor certainty

Estimated, remembered, simulated and API-derived values remain labelled. Missing measured data stays missing.

## No hidden semantic claim

Birds, rain, wind or machines may shape music. The system does not claim they encode a political, spiritual or linguistic message.

## Voice and consent

Recorded human voices require an appropriate consent and publication basis. A public canonical example should prefer derived features or cleared media over identifiable private speech.

## Location privacy

Exact coordinates are optional. Public sessions should use the least precise location needed to support reproducibility and artistic context.

## Evaluation scope

Listening scores evaluate the artistic system. They do not establish scientific causality between environmental variables and emotion.
