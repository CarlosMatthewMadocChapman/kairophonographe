# Method

## 1. Unit of work: the situated session

The fundamental object is not a genre prompt. It is a time-bounded session containing environmental observations and their provenance.

A session may be rich or sparse. Missing inputs are allowed. Unknown data stays unknown.

## 2. Force extraction

The engine reads four evidence families:

- **matter**: surfaces, geology, vegetation, built materials;
- **time**: local hour, season, evolution during the session;
- **weather**: temperature, humidity, pressure, wind, precipitation, cloud/light;
- **sound**: density, periodicity, dynamic variability and identifiable source families.

Each input affects specific musical targets. This keeps the system inspectable.

## 3. Weighted translation

Mappings create biases, not total decisions. Example: temperature contributes a tempo bias, while sound event density, wind, precipitation, season and the kinetic regime also affect final tempo. This prevents a single sensor from reducing a place to one musical cliché.

## 4. Kinetic arbitration

The reference engine chooses among interpretable regimes such as walking ostinato, living mechanical, active weather, montage/rupture and contemplative breathing. The regime constrains the final tempo range and pulse behaviour.

## 5. Music ADN gestures

A session should use a small number of audible actions: reveal, deepen, connect, stretch, condense, make breathable, protect silence. A gesture is valid only when it changes structure, density, attack, space, timbre, pulse or vocality.

## 6. Resonance palettes

Palettes are response families. They do not represent cultures or countries. The engine chooses them from physical and temporal forces.

## 7. Vocal transmutation

Vocality is optional. Dense birds, insects, wind, water or recurrent machinery may support non-lexical human transformation when the timing or contour can be preserved. The source is never claimed to contain human language.

## 8. Rendering

The reference engine returns a structured music specification. A separate renderer may turn this specification into a score, synthesis graph, live process or generative-music prompt.

## 9. Listening closes the loop

A mapping becomes stronger only after rendered results are evaluated. Useful accidents are recorded as candidates, then tested before promotion to the stable layer.
